The **Week 5 assignment requirement** is to take a **small OpenRAM-generated SRAM macro** and perform **complete physical verification and post-layout characterization**. It assumes that you already know basic SRAM blocks and have generated an SRAM macro.

The assignment is essentially asking you to prove:

> "The SRAM generated by OpenRAM is physically correct, functionally correct, and its real silicon behaviour matches the timing/power models."

---
